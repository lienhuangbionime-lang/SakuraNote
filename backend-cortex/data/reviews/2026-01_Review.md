## 🧗 Challenges & Roadblocks
*   **Technical Instability & "Time Black Holes":** Faced recurring technical difficulties with AI generative tools (version conflicts, loss of control, vendor lock-in risks) and significant setbacks like accidental cloud environment deletions (NestJS project) and Vercel quota limitations, forcing project resets and consuming valuable time. Data linking and UI interruptions during system revamps proved frustrating.
*   **Cognitive Biases & Self-Imposed Barriers:** Grappled with various cognitive biases including "expert blind spot," "confirmation bias," "hindsight bias," "effort justification bias," and "complexity bias." This led to procrastination (e.g., delaying resume submission, consistent reading), over-optimization of tools at the expense of core tasks, and mistaking "system completeness" for actual progress.
*   **AI Collaboration Friction:** AI-generated code often lacked structural integrity and deviated from intended architecture, requiring extensive human review and "prompt engineering" to ensure system respect and consistency. Experienced "critique fatigue" when AI feedback felt overly judgmental, triggering a need for prompt recalibration.
*   **Work-Life Balance Strain:** The intense focus on system development frequently encroached upon dedicated family time, leading to direct feedback from the partner and feelings of self-reproach. External life events, such as child separation anxiety or illness, also interrupted focused work periods.
*   **Data & Information Gaps:** Encountered roadblocks in the stock system validation due to missing crucial industry and institutional investor data, highlighting the limitations of current tools and data sources.

## 💡 Insights & Lessons Learned
*   **Antifragility through Hybrid Systems:** Full automation in AI-driven development is often fragile; a hybrid approach that blends AI-powered generation with human control and iterative refinement is more robust. Challenges are not failures but signals to strengthen the system.
*   **Define AI's Role as "Executor," Not "Critic":** AI tools are most effective as "executors" or "renderers," not as supervisors or critics. Effective "prompt engineering" is critical for aligning AI output with user intent and avoiding cognitive biases in feedback. "Vibe Coding" offers a promising path to start development from visual state rather than from scratch, allowing humans to focus on iteration.
*   **"Capture, Connect, Emerge" for Knowledge Systems:** The diary system's evolution underscored that knowledge management thrives not just on linear recording, but on capturing ideas quickly and building relational graphs to connect disparate thoughts, allowing insights to emerge.
*   **Prioritize "Why" Over "How" & Embrace MVPs:** Before diving into complex technical implementations, rigorously validate product assumptions and structural needs (e.g., understanding market dynamics before building a voice accounting app). Combat "analysis paralysis" and "complexity bias" by focusing on Minimal Viable State Machines (MVSMs) or "working skeletons" to break ground and iterate.
*   **Protect Family & Self-Input as Core Assets:** Family connection is a central "module" of life, requiring conscious design and protected time, not merely efficient management. Consistent intellectual input (reading) is vital for long-term growth and should not be replaced by the immediate gratification of AI output; it's the "input capital" for future breakthroughs.
*   **Communication Entropy & Clear Protocols:** Friction in human-AI communication or between system components is a valuable signal for needing clearer protocols and establishing "logic checkpoints" to maintain system integrity.
